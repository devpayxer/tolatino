# How voting works

_Clave: votos. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

In the Community, the most useful things rise with neighbors' votes. Nobody pays to be on top.

## What changed
- First version: how votes count, polls and what is not allowed.

## How to vote
- Posts and comments have votes; replies only get up votes.
- One vote per person on each post and each comment. The same tap removes it; the other one changes it.
- You do not vote on your own things: others do.

## What it orders
- The feed and the threads are ordered by most voted (and recent), or by newest if you choose it.
- If the person who asked marks «Helped me», that answer goes on top. If nobody marked one, the most voted (with 5 votes or more) goes on top.
- The only post that goes on top without votes is the official To'Latino account of your city, pinned and marked as official.
- Votes order things; they do not hide anything. What is hidden is hidden by the [Community rules](/reglas), with a notice and an appeal.

## One phone, one vote
If you vote from the same phone with another account, the vote is saved but it does not count or move the order. Voting with several
accounts or paying for votes breaks the rules.

## Polls
You vote once and it cannot be changed. Percentages show after you vote. When it closes, everyone sees the result.

## Points
Up votes give points and ranks in the Community. If something is hidden by the rules, the points it gave are taken away; if you win the
appeal, they come back.
