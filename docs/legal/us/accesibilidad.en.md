# Accessibility

_Clave: accesibilidad. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

We want To'Latino to work well with any phone, eyesight or way of navigating: by touch, with a keyboard, with a screen reader or with large text.

## What changed
- First version of the accessibility statement.

## What we aim for
We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA, the international web accessibility standard, and we check it automatically every time we change something.
This applies to the web and to the four apps (To'Latino, Negocios, Repartidor and Puerta).

## What we already take care of
- Everything works with a keyboard: ordering, booking, buying tickets, listing a business.
- Every button has a name for the screen reader (VoiceOver on iPhone, TalkBack on Android).
- You can see where you are: the active item has a visible outline.
- Errors are announced to the screen reader.
- Spanish and English on every screen.
- To'Latino follows your phone's text size.

## What we are still improving
Some light gray text does not yet have the contrast the guidelines ask for, and some controls are smaller than recommended for a finger. We
are fixing them. If you cannot use something, tell us.

## How to tell us
Write to us in [Support](/soporte) and tell us what you were trying to do and with which tool (screen reader, keyboard, voice control). We
treat it as a bug and reply.
