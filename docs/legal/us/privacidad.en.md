# Privacy policy

_Clave: privacidad. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

What data To'Latino keeps, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what you can do with it. Further down, each piece of data and what happens to it when you delete your account come from the real list of what we keep.

## What changed
- First version: it comes from the data inventory and the map of what is exported and deleted.

## What we do not do
- We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not use the phone's advertising ID.
- There is no third-party advertising on To'Latino. We do not sell your data or share it for ads.
- Analytics are our own: they never leave To'Latino.
- We never ask for your immigration status or your social security number.

## What data we keep
- **Your account:** your phone, your name and, if you add it, your email.
- **What you do:** your orders, appointments, bookings, tickets and payments; your delivery addresses if you save them.
- **What you post:** reviews, posts, comments, photos and messages.
- **Your location:** only when you tap «Use my location», to show you what is nearby. Never in the background. If you are a driver, your
  location is shared only during an active delivery.
- **Your phone for To'Latino:** a To'Latino number for this device (not the manufacturer's or the advertising one).
- **How the app is used:** which screens open and what fails, to improve it, without ads.

The full list, item by item, is below in «The data we use and why».

## Data we take extra care of
- **Health:** To'Latino is not a doctor's office. We do not keep medical records or diagnoses; your note for an appointment is encrypted and
  deleted 30 days after the appointment. Notifications about a medical appointment do not say the office or the reason.
- **Children:** for an enrollment we keep the minimum (first name, age or grade), encrypted, and it is deleted 30 days after it ends. A child's
  name never appears in notifications, searches, analytics or El Compa.
- **Cards:** your card number goes to Stripe; To'Latino keeps only the brand and the last 4 digits.

## What we use it for
- To make To'Latino work: so the business serves you, your order arrives and your ticket gets in.
- For your account and your safety: signing in, stopping abuse and fake accounts, resolving disputes.
- To notify you about your things (your order, your appointment, your messages), the way you choose in My account › Notifications.
- To improve To'Latino with our own analytics.

## Who it is shared with
- **The business** you deal with gets only what it needs to serve you (your name, what you ordered and, for a delivery, your address and your
  phone for that delivery).
- **The services that work for To'Latino**, only with what their part needs:
  - Stripe: card payments and payouts to businesses.
  - Telnyx: text messages (your sign-in code and SMS notifications).
  - Meta (WhatsApp): only if you turn on «Talk to El Compa by WhatsApp and SMS».
  - Amazon Web Services (SES): email, if you add your email.
  - Anthropic: El Compa, the AI assistant. What you write arrives already masked (no cards, social security, codes or passwords).
  - Apple and Google: phone notifications and tickets in Wallet.

- **What never leaves To'Latino:** photos, the «See translation» translator, photo review, maps, address lookup and analytics run on

## How long it is kept
- Your account and your things: while you have the account.
- What you search: 90 days. Sessions that no longer work: 400 days. Sign-in codes: 7 days.
- A delivery's location: 14 days; after that only the distance and the time.
- Our own analytics: 13 months. App crashes and errors: 90 days.
- Conversations with El Compa: 12 months. Your Support cases: while open and one year after.
- Ticket scans at the door: 13 months with detail; after that only the numbers.
- What the law requires us to keep about a payment or a receipt is kept as long as it requires, without your name if you deleted your account.

## What you can do
- **Download your data:** in My account › Settings › Privacy › Download my data. We build a file with your things; you can request it once
  every 7 days and download it once within 24 hours.
- **Delete your account:** in My account › Settings › Delete my account, on the web and in the four apps. After 30 days your things are
  deleted; what the law or money requires us to keep stays without your name or your phone. Below, «What happens to each piece of data when
  you delete your account» says what is done with each one and why.
- **Correct:** you change your name, your photo, your email and your addresses in My account.
- **Notifications, sessions and permissions:** you turn off each type of notification in My account › Notifications; you close your sessions
  and your passkeys in My account › Privacy; you say no to the camera, location or notifications, and the app asks again only when it uses them.

## Security
Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS). The most sensitive data (health notes, children's data, notification tokens) is stored encrypted. The
To'Latino team sees only what their role allows, and every time someone opens something sensitive it is recorded who and why.
