# Fair housing

_Clave: vivienda-justa. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

A property on To'Latino is advertised for what it is, never for who may live in it. Equal housing opportunity.

## What changed
- First version: what cannot be advertised, how To'Latino checks it and the exceptions.

## What the law says
The US Fair Housing Act forbids discrimination in renting or selling housing because of race, color, religion, sex, national origin,
disability or familial status (for example, having children). Each state's laws may protect more; what applies in Pennsylvania is below.

## What To'Latino does not let you post
- A property that prefers or excludes someone because of who they are or who lives with them: race, color, religion, sex, orientation or
  identity, disability, familial status, national origin, ancestry or age.
- Phrases such as «no children», «adults only», «ideal for a couple», «Christians only», «for Hispanics» or «no Section 8», in Spanish or in
  English. Section 8 can be **accepted**; excluding it is never advertised.
- On saving, To'Latino checks the name, the description, what is required, the lease and the other payments. If it finds a phrase like that,
  the property is not saved and it says which one to remove.

## What you can advertise
The property's features (bedrooms, utilities, pets, furnished) and the papers that are accepted («No social security», «With ITIN»): which
papers are accepted, never who.

## Service and support animals
They are not pets: they are always accepted, even if the property says «no pets».

## Housing for older persons
Only with the declaration of the exemption the law allows; it opens a case the To'Latino team
reviews; if it is not confirmed, the property is hidden with the reason.
