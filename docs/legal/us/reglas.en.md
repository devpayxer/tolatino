# Community rules

_Clave: reglas. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

The Community is the place to ask and recommend in your city. Nothing is bought here: neighbor votes set the order.

## What changed
- First version of the Community rules, with how things are reported, hidden and appealed.

## The rules
1. Talk about your city and what happens here.
2. Recommend from experience, never your own business. Your business posts in its Updates.
3. No sales, raffles or chain messages.
4. Do not post other people's phones, addresses or photos without their permission.
5. Respect always: no insults or harassment.
6. An emergency is not asked here: call 911.

To enter the Community you accept these rules and tell us you are 18 or older.

## How they are kept
- **Reporting:** any neighbor can report a post, a comment, a profile or a conversation. What was reported stops showing for the person who
  reported it and a person on the team reviews it within 48 hours. Nobody knows who reported.
- **What the automatic rules check:** repeated texts, hate or scam words, signs of where someone lives and photos. They flag it so a person
  decides; they do not hide anything on their own. Phones, emails and private numbers are not posted: they are stopped while typing.
- **Auto-hide:** if five different people report the same thing within 24 hours, it is hidden while we review it, and the person who posted
  it is notified.
- **Daily limits:** the same for everyone, to stop spam.

## If To'Latino hides something of yours
- We tell you why. You can still see what was hidden. Nothing is deleted.
- Sanctions go from a warning to a mute, a posting block, a suspension or, in the most serious cases, closing the account. Each one says until
  when and why.
- **Appeal:** once, up to 14 days after the notice, from the notice or My account › Moderation notices. Another person on the team reviews it,
  never the one who decided, and we reply within 72 hours. That answer is final.

## If someone is in danger
If it is an emergency, call 911. If someone writes that they are thinking of hurting themselves, we show them the 988 line (call or text, also
in Spanish).

## What you post and the law
What each neighbor posts belongs to whoever posts it. To'Latino moderates it with these rules and may hide it, but does not write it and a person does
not review it before it goes out (photos do go through an automatic review first). [pending: the text about Section 230 and about harassment and defamation, to be written with the lawyer.]
