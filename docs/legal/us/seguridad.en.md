# Safety

_Clave: seguridad. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

What we do and what we ask of you so everything goes well.

## What changed
- First version: paying inside To'Latino, what you never share and what to do if something is wrong.

## Pay inside To'Latino
When you pay by card inside To'Latino, the money is held until the business delivers, and if it does not you can open «Help» on your order,
appointment or ticket. What you pay outside (for example, by transfer to a number someone sent you in a message) we cannot review or refund.

## Never share
- The code you get by text message. To'Latino never asks you for it by phone, by message or through El Compa.
- Card numbers, passwords or your social security number in a message. El Compa masks them if you type them.

## Your account
- You sign in with a one-time code that expires in 10 minutes, or with your passkey.
- In My account › Settings › Privacy you see your open sessions and close the ones you do not recognize.
- If someone got into your account, write to us in [Support](/soporte).

## If something is wrong
- Report the post, the comment, the profile or the business. The team reviews it and tells you what was done.
- If it is an emergency, call 911. To'Latino is not an emergency service.

## How we look after To'Latino
Everything travels encrypted. The most sensitive data is stored encrypted. The team sees only what their role allows and what they open is
recorded. We review security every time we change something that touches accounts, money or personal data.
