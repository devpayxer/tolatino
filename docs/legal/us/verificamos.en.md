# How we verify a business

_Clave: verificamos. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

The verification badge and verified licenses are given by the To'Latino team after a review. They cannot be bought.

## What changed
- First version: what we check for the badge and for each license, and what we never ask for.

## The verification badge
The business requests it from its dashboard. A person on the team checks three things before giving it:
- That the business exists in its city: the address and the photos match.
- That whoever requests it represents the business (for example, with the store's phone).
- That the page says what the business really does.

If something is missing, we ask for one more detail; if it is not approved, we say why and the business can request it again. The team can
remove the badge if something stops being true, and the business is notified.

## Licenses
- When a job requires a license, the business page shows which one, who issued it, its number and what that license allows and does not
  allow.
- The team looks it up in the official registry and marks it **Verified** (with the date), **Expired** or **Not found**. A verified license is
  checked again every 3 months, and a new number goes back to review.
- The link to the official registry comes from To'Latino's list, never from what the business types, so you can check it yourself.

## What we never ask for
No personal ID documents and no immigration status. Not from the business and not from you.

## What the badge does not say
The badge says the business exists and that whoever runs it represents it. It is not a guarantee of the result of a service. If something is
wrong, report the business or open «Help» on your order or appointment.
