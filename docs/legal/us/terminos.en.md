- The price you see before sending is the total, with the fees and taxes that apply.
- Card payments are processed by Stripe. To'Latino does not keep your card number: only the brand and the last 4 digits.
- **Held** means your card reserves the amount and nothing is charged yet; **charged**, that it was charged; **released**, that the hold was

- Each order, appointment, booking, ticket, layaway or plan shows its cancellation and refund policy **before** you send it. That policy is
