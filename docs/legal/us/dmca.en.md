# Copyright (DMCA)

_Clave: dmca. Jurisdicción: us. Idioma: en. Fecha del texto: 14 nov 2026. Estado del texto: BORRADOR — pendiente de revisión legal._

What to do if something posted on To'Latino uses your work (a photo, a text, a logo) without your permission, and what to do if something of yours was removed by mistake.

## What changed

## If someone uses your work without permission
Send us a notice with:
1. The work that is yours (or of whoever you represent) and that you believe was copied.
2. Where it is on To'Latino: the link to the post, the review, the photo or the business page.
3. Your name, your address, your phone and your email.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by you, your agent or the law.
5. A statement that what you write is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you own the work or act on its owner's behalf.
6. Your signature (your typed name is fine).

**Where to send it:** [pending: To'Latino's designated agent registered with the US Copyright Office, with their address and email.] Until it
is registered, write to us in [Support](/soporte) with «Copyright» in the subject.

## What we do
We review the notice. If it is complete, we hide what was reported and notify the person who posted it, with the reason and what they can do.
Nothing is deleted: it stays hidden.

## If something of yours was removed by mistake
You can send a counter-notice with:
1. What was removed and where it was.
2. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification.
3. Your name, your address and your phone, and that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address.
4. Your signature.

We pass it on to whoever sent the notice. [pending: the timeframes to show it again, to be confirmed with the lawyer.]

## Repeat infringers
We close the account of anyone who posts other people's work without permission again and again.
