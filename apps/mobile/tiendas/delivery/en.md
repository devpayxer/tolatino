Deliveries for the business you drive for: the order, the address and the map.

# Texto promocional
See your deliveries for the day, open the address in Maps or Waze and mark each one when you deliver it.

# Descripción
To'Latino Repartidor is for people who deliver orders for a business on To'Latino. The business invites you with a code and you sign in with your phone.

What you can do in the app:
- See the deliveries the business assigns to you, with what each order has and how it is paid.
- Open the address in Maps or Waze with one tap.
- Mark "Picked up" and "Delivered".
- Get a notification when a delivery is assigned to you.
- See your deliveries for the day even without signal.

Your location: the app shares it only while you have an active delivery, also with the screen off, so the customer and the business can see where the order is. It stops when you mark "Delivered". It never asks for "always".

Your data: we do not use advertising or track what you do in other apps. Delete your account in My account › Settings.
