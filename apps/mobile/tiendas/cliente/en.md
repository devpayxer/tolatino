To'Latino

# Subtítulo
Latino businesses in your city

# Descripción corta
Latino businesses, orders, bookings, events and Community. Spanish and English.

# Texto promocional
Order, book and buy tickets at the Latino businesses of Hazleton, Allentown, Wilkes-Barre, Scranton and Reading. In Spanish and English.

# Descripción
To'Latino brings the Latino businesses of your city together in one app: restaurants, barbershops and salons, doctors, auto shops, churches, stores, events and more. It works in Hazleton, Allentown, Wilkes-Barre, Scranton and Reading, in Spanish and English.

What you can do in the app:
- Find a business by what you need and see its hours, photos, reviews and whether it is open now.
- Order food for pickup or delivery and follow the order to your door.
- Book an appointment or a table, with a notification when the business confirms it.
- Buy event tickets, add them to Apple Wallet and open them offline at the door.
- Message the business and get its replies in the app.
- Your city's Community: neighborhood notices, questions, deals and what is happening today.
- El Compa, the assistant that helps you with an order, an appointment or a question, and connects you with a person on the team when needed.

Your account is your phone: sign in with a code sent by text. Pay by card in the app when the business allows it, or at the store.

Your data: we do not use advertising or track what you do in other apps. Delete your account anytime in My account › Settings.

Help and Support are inside the app and at tolatino.com.
