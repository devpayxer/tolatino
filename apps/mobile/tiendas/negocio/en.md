# Descripción
To'Latino Negocios is your business dashboard on To'Latino, on your phone. It is the same account and the same dashboard you use on the web.

What you can do in the app:
- Receive orders, appointments, reservations and requests with an instant notification, with sound, and open them from the notification.
- See your day in Today: what is coming in, what you are preparing and what already went out.
- Answer your customers' messages.
- Change your menu, services, prices, hours and photos.
- Read a coupon code or scan tickets at your event door with the camera.
- See your card payouts and your team.
- Ask El Compa how each part of the dashboard works.

Sign in with your phone and a code sent by text. Your plan shows in Payouts and plan.

Your data: we do not use advertising or track what you do in other apps. Delete your account in My account › Settings.

Help and Support for businesses are inside the app.
