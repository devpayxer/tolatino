# Nombre
To'Latino Puerta

# Subtítulo
Scan tickets at the door

# Descripción corta
Scan To'Latino tickets at your event door, even without signal.

# Palabras clave
door,tickets,scan,QR,event,entry,guest list,capacity,check-in,latino

# Texto promocional
Scan tickets at the door with the screen on, even when the venue loses signal.

# Descripción
To'Latino Puerta is for the people who welcome guests at the door of a To'Latino event. The organizer invites you with the door code and you sign in with your phone.

What you can do in the app:
- Scan each ticket's QR code with the camera, with the screen always on.
- Find someone by name or order number.
- See the list, the capacity and who came in through each door.
- Assign tables and log incidents.
- Keep scanning without signal: the signed list stays on the phone and everything uploads when the network is back.
- Pin the screen on Android so nobody leaves the Door.

The camera only reads the QR code: it does not save photos or video.

Your data: we do not use advertising or track what you do in other apps. Delete your account in My account › Settings.
